Vegan Pesto Pasta

Photograph by Luca Nebuloni, licensed under CC BY 2.0.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb Pasta of your choice (I recommend farfalle or spaghetti)
  • 1/2 cup cashews or pine nuts
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2/3 cup olive oil
  • 1/3 cup nutritional yeast
  • 2 cups fresh basil leaves
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cook pasta according to directions on box.
  2. While pasta is cooking, add all other ingredients to a food processer and blend until smooth. If mixture is too thick, add water or olive oil as needed.
  3. When pasta is finished cooking, drain and add pesto sauce to pot of cooked pasta.
  4. Serve with a sprinkle of parmesan on top.
