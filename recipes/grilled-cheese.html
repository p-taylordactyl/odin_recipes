Grilled Cheese

Photo by Gio Bartlett on Unsplash

Description

The grilled cheese sandwich is a simple, classic recipe that's easy to both make and enjoy. WIth few ingredients and hardly any steps, it's a great choice when you want comfort food without putting in too much effort. Personally, I recommend using a cast-iron pan for best results, but really any pan will get the job done. The humble grilled cheese is forgiving like that. As such, you can also use pretty much any bread or cheese you want. White bread and cheddar are the classic, but I've also had great results with ciabatta and gruyere. Feel free to throw in some cooked bacon for extra protein as well. This recipe is simply a starting point, a canvas for you to begin the quest for your perfect grilled cheese.

Pro Tip: If you want a crisper, more caramelized crust, try making this recipe with mayo in place of the butter.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices white bread
  • 3 tablespoons butter, divided
  • 4 slices cheddar cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat a nonstick skillet over medium heat.
  2. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
  3. Place one slice of bread in the hot skillet, butter side down. Top with two slices of cheese.
  4. Place one slice of bread on top of the cheese, butter side up.
  5. Cook until lightly browned on one side, flip and continue cooking until both sides are browned and cheese is melted.
  6. Repeat steps 2-4 with the remaining bread and cheese slices and serve.
